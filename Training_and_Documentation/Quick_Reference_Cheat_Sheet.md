## 🕐 Update Store Hours (5 minutes)

1. Go to **github.com** → Sign in
2. Click **Store-Website**
3. Click **pages** folder → **contact.html**
4. Click pencil icon ✏️
5. Search for "Hours" (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F)
6. Update the time
7. Write what you changed → Click **Commit changes**
8. Wait 2 minutes → Check your website!

---

## 📞 Update Phone Number (5 minutes)

1. Follow steps 1-4 above
2. Search for "Phone" (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F)
3. Update phone number in TWO places:
   - Contact section
   - Footer section (scroll to bottom)
4. Write what you changed → Click **Commit changes**

## ⚠️ Important Tips

✅ **DO:**

❌ **DON'T:**
