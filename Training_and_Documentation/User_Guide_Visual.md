# 🕐 Slide 6: How to Update Store Hours

## What You're Updating:

Your website shows store hours in **3 places:**
1. Home page
2. Contact page
3. Footer (bottom of all pages)

**Good news!** You only need to update **one file** and it changes everywhere! ✨

---

# 🕐 Slide 7: Update Store Hours - Step 1

## Navigate to the Contact Page File:

1. Click on the **"pages"** folder

2. Click on **"contact.html"**

3. You'll see the code for your contact page

4. Click the **pencil icon** (✏️) in the top right that says "Edit this file"

---

# 🕐 Slide 8: Update Store Hours - Step 2

## Find the Hours Section:

Press **Ctrl+F** (Windows) or **Cmd+F** (Mac) to search

Type: `Hours`

You'll find this section:
```html
<h4>🕒 Hours</h4>
<p>Monday - Sunday: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM<br>
<em>Open Daily</em></p>
```

---

# 🕐 Slide 9: Update Store Hours - Step 3

## Make Your Changes:

**Example:** If you want to change closing time to 11:00 PM:

**BEFORE:**
```html
<p>Monday - Sunday: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM<br>
```

**AFTER:**
```html
<p>Monday - Sunday: 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM<br>
```

> ⚠️ **Important:** Only change the TIME, not the other parts like `<p>` or `<br>`

---

# 🕐 Slide 10: Update Store Hours - Step 4

## Save Your Changes:

1. Scroll to the bottom of the page

2. You'll see a box that says **"Commit changes"**

3. In the text box, type what you changed:
   - Example: "Updated store closing time"

4. Click the green **"Commit changes"** button

**That's it!** Your website is now updated! 🎉
